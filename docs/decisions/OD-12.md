# OD-12 — Stock-warning thresholds and inventory authority

**Status:** APPLIED (default)
**Owner:** Product
**Needed by:** Phase 2
**Blocks:** restock detection, batch preview warnings

## Question

When should staff be warned that stock is short, and which source is authoritative?

## Applied answer

**The inventory source is authoritative.** This app does not compute stock from
anything — it mirrors what the source says. If the source says a variant has 3 units,
this app believes it has 3 units.

**Stock warning threshold:** warn when
`stock_after < count(eligible waiting list)` — that is, when the stock that would
remain after fulfilling the waiting list is smaller than the number of eligible people
waiting. This is the number that actually matters: it is the count of people who will
be disappointed, not a fixed buffer.

The warning appears in the **batch preview**, before the batch is confirmed, so an
owner sees it while they can still decide.

## Why not a fixed buffer

A fixed buffer (say 10 units) is wrong at both ends: for a cheap item with 500 waiters
it under-warns, and for an expensive item with 3 waiters it over-warns. The eligible
waiting-list count is the quantity that matters.

## Granularity caveat

This depends on OD-01. If inventory is tracked per product rather than per variant, the
count must be over the product's total and RP-03 ("variants are never mixed") cannot be
honoured.
