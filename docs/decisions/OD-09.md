# OD-09 — FAQ ownership, editing and review workflow

**Status:** APPLIED (default)
**Owner:** Product
**Needed by:** Phase 4
**Blocks:** FAQ registry, zero-token route

## Question

Who owns FAQ entries, who may edit them, and how does quality stay high?

## Applied answer

- **Owners:** the `owner` and `admin` roles. Staff read FAQs and use them in chat, but
  do not author or edit them.
- **Every entry has a named owner** (`faq_entries.owner_user_id`) and a
  `review_due_at` date.
- **Overdue entries are flagged** — surfaced in the dashboard as needing review, and
  excluded from the highest-trust tier of the matcher until reviewed.
- **Aliases** are separate rows (`faq_aliases`) so a Banglish or Bangla spelling of the
  same question resolves to one entry without duplicating the entry.

## Why an owner and a review date

The zero-token route (a question answered straight from the FAQ registry without
calling the model) is only trustworthy if someone is responsible for keeping the
registry true. An entry nobody owns drifts out of date, and then the system confidently
answers from stale text.
