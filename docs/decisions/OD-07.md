# OD-07 — Consent capture, retention and erasure

**Status:** APPLIED (default) — confirm with legal before launch
**Owner:** Product + Legal
**Needed by:** Phase 1
**Blocks:** consent flow, export gating, retention job

## Question

How is consent captured, how long is data kept, and what happens on an erasure request?

## Applied answer

**Consent capture.** Consent is recorded per customer, per channel, per purpose
(`review_request`, `restock_notification`, `own_ads_promotion`, `research_export`).
Each record carries the channel, the purpose, the timestamp, the source, and the
message that carried it. Consent for one purpose is not consent for another — a
customer who agreed to a review request has not agreed to an ad.

**Retention.**

| Data | Retention | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Reviews and review replies | 24 months | enough to evidence a solicitation |
| Chats | 24 months | same |
| Consent records | **indefinitely** | the consent record *is* the audit trail; deleting it destroys the proof |
| Identity links | while linked, plus 12 months | reconciliation window |
| Audit log | 24 months | operational and legal trail |

**Erasure.** An erasure request deletes the customer's PII — name, phone, email, message
content — and **keeps the consent record**, with the linked identifiers hashed. This is
the standard shape: forget the person, keep the record that you were told to forget them.

`legal_hold` on a customer record suspends erasure.

## Open item

The retention periods above are the default applied to the build. They are legal
positions, not engineering ones, and must be confirmed by whoever owns compliance
before the app takes real customer data. The implementation is a retention job driven by
these values, so changing them is a config change.
