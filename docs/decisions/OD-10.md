# OD-10 — AI provider, cost caps, retention and redaction

**Status:** OPEN — provider deferred to Phase 5; caps and redaction applied now
**Owner:** Product + Engineering
**Needed by:** Phase 4
**Blocks:** chat routing, usage metering

## Question

Which LLM provider and model, what are the spend caps, how long are chats kept, and
what is redacted before the call?

## Applied answer

**Provider:** deferred. The chat module talks to an `LLMPort` interface, not to a
vendor SDK. The provider is chosen at Phase 5 and swapping it does not touch the
routers.

**Cost caps.** Two levels, both enforced before the call:

- per request — a request exceeding the per-request cap is refused, not truncated
- per business per day — a business that hits its daily cap is refused until the next
  day, and the refusal is visible in the dashboard

Every call is recorded in `ai_usage_events` with route, model, input/output tokens,
estimated cost, latency, tools invoked and a prompt hash. The prompt hash lets two
identical prompts be recognised without storing the prompt's PII.

**Redaction.** PII is redacted **before** the provider call, not after. Names, phone
numbers, emails and addresses are replaced with stable placeholders
(`[CUSTOMER_NAME]`, `[PHONE]`, …) so the provider never receives them. The answer is
then re-hydrated with the real values before display.

**Chat retention:** 24 months, matching reviews and chats under OD-07.
